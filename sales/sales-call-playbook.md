# Strategy Session Playbook - built from the closes in January, March and April 2026

Source: every Fathom recording on the tracker for the three highest revenue months -
April 2026 ($62,000), March 2026 ($55,000) and January 2026 ($44,000). That was 24 recordings
of calls marked WON, plus 6 LOST calls from the same months used as a comparison. Every
transcript was read in full. All quotes are your words from those recordings, lightly cleaned of
filler, with the family name and timestamp so you can go back and listen.

Three closes from those months have no recording on the tracker (Matías Pereira, Layla Mike,
Mya Taylor) and are not included.

## The 7 things that separate a close from a loss

### 1. Every decision-maker is on the call - and you check before the pitch, not after the price
- **On the closes,** you asked up front.
  - Barrales [00:57]: "Does anyone else need to be on the call who's going to be part of making a
    decision, that it would be helpful for them to hear what it looks like?"
    Then [01:51]: "Are you sure? Because I want to... not have to relay the information twice."
  - Zimerman [03:07]: "Is mom around? Does she want to hop on the call so she can get all the
    info too?"
  - Zimerman [00:57], about the athlete: "He's gonna need to be on the call, for sure."
- **Brooke** [33:04]: when mom said "something we have to talk to Daddy about," you didn't book
  a follow-up. You got dad patched back in on the spot: "Do you want to call him real quick and
  see if we can patch him back on?" It closed on that call, paid in full.
- **On the 6 lost calls you never asked who else decides** (0 of 6).
  - Gaby's mom controlled half the money and was not on the call. That surfaced at [59:27]. You
    said at [1:01:28]: "I usually do, that's always best."
  - Alejandra's real funders were the grandparents [1:08:00].
- **Stoecklin** (January, $8K) did not close on the call. The mom who handles the money wasn't
  there - the dad said "it's just not my decision."

### 2. The athlete says out loud they are all in - before you say the price
- Almost every close had a commitment gate right before the price, aimed at the athlete, then
  the parent.
  - McKenzie [24:31]: "On a scale of 1 to 10, 1 being like, nah, 10 being like 100%, like how
    committed right now are you to this process of making this dream happen?"
  - McKenzie [24:45]: "Okay, tell me where that hesitation is."
  - McKenzie [25:35]: "Because I really can't move forward if we're not like at a 10."
  - Payton went from "a seven or eight" to "a nine or a ten" after [42:01]: "Is the hesitation just
    like the fear of like what it entails, like the unknown, or is it like you don't know if that's
    what you want?"
- When you skipped this or did it after the price, it didn't close on the call. Stoecklin was
  the only January $8K where the commitment check came after the number.

### 3. The parent names the pain in their own words, and you summarise it back as the product
- Barrales [21:31]: "What you guys really need is the clear guidance, the blueprint, and the map
  to get it done. That's kind of what I'm getting here. Would you agree?"
- The Junior Prep closes all had the parent say some version of "accountability from someone who
  isn't mom and dad":
  - Genevieve [20:34]
  - India [21:26]
  - Gabby [40:05]: "accountability piece coming from someone who's not mom and dad is really
    helpful."

### 4. Price once, flat, no justification - then straight to the money question
- Same words on nearly every call:
  "So you guys want to know what it takes to get started?" ... "The investment is $8,000."
  Then, without filling the silence: "Is that something you're able to do?"
- On the closes the next question was "You got the card on hand you want to use?"
  - Payton [46:10], Erin [36:17], Brooke [39:50], Trinity [35:21].
- The payment link went into the chat or by message while you were still on, and you started
  onboarding while they paid.

### 5. Move the payment structure, never the price
- Split pay produced the yes on Arevalo, Barrales, McKenzie, Trinity and Garza.
  - Arevalo [37:54]: "What I can do is a split pay... half down now and then another half in 30
    days. Would that work for you?"
- Erin went to a 6-payment internal plan. Beth went to a $1,500 first payment.
- The question that sorts the objection, used on Barrales [36:51], Erin [33:16] and Hope [30:50]:
  "Is that more than you were expecting, or more than you're able to do?"

### 6. Ask about money before the pitch
- None of the 6 lost calls touched money before the price. Hope's mom expected "a few hundred
  dollars every month" [32:06] - that was only discovered after $8,000 was on the table.
- On several closes you surfaced it early.
  - Arevalo [15:55]: "Currently, do you guys like, do we have a path to pay for that to make that
    happen?" This came after looking up tuition and average scholarship. The dad answered "the
    financials are okay," 11 minutes before the price.
  - Seeleigh [21:10] and Payton [18:07]: "What is your guys' current plan to pay for college
    right now?"

### 7. When they won't decide on the call, book the decision - don't wait for it
- On the closes that happened later:
  - Zimerman [43:56]: "When do you guys think you'll be able to sit down as a family and talk
    about it?" You offered 8pm the same day, then booked Monday.
  - Seeleigh [58:55]: "So then when should I put it on my end to check in with you guys to just
    make sure that nothing falls through the cracks on my end?"
  - Stoecklin: you moved the existing calendar invite to Wednesday 6:30.
  - Rappe: you put the kickoff on the calendar before the yes.
- The line that got a deposit instead of a "let me talk to her dad" - Beth [43:25]:
  "As soon as we, after a call within 24 hours, we forget 50% of what was discussed on the call.
  So I'm wondering if... we just move forward with that first payment, just so we could get
  everything started on our end."

## THE CALL - play by play, in order

Target length about 45-50 minutes, with the price at about minute 30-40. Wording is yours, from
the recordings. The call name after each line shows where it came from.

### 0. Open (0-2 min)
1. "Can you hear me and see me okay?"
2. "Tell me how to say your name so I don't embarrass myself." (Barrales)
3. "You guys are in [state], right? What part?" - find a personal tie (club, hometown, faith,
   homeschool, a mutual player).
4. "And then [grade] with a [GPA], correct?" (Barrales, Zimerman)
5. "Right now still a good time for you guys to chat? You'll have about 45 minutes?"
   (Barrales, Zimerman)
6. **Decision-maker check - do not skip:**
   - "Does anyone else need to be on the call who's going to be part of making a decision,
     that it would be helpful for them to hear what it looks like?" (Barrales)
   - If someone is missing: "Does mom/dad want to pop in so they get the overview too? I want to
     not have to relay the information twice." (Barrales, Seeleigh)
   - If the athlete is missing: "[Athlete] is gonna need to be on the call, for sure." (Zimerman)

### 1. Frame (by minute 2)
7. "What I want to do, guys, is deep dive on your situation, understand where you're at and what
   your goals are, and then as long as I know for a fact I can help you, I'll offer you a spot in
   the program and show you how it works. If I don't feel like we're the right fit, I'll just
   point you guys in the best direction I see fit for your situation. Does that sound good?"
   (near word for word on most closes)

### 2. Why they're here
8. "Why don't you start by telling me, what's the main reason we're on the call today?"
9. "How long have you been wanting to play college soccer?" (McKenzie, Trinity)

### 3. Current situation - athlete first, then parent
10. "What is your current training schedule looking like? Give me the full picture." (most calls)
11. "So we're getting on the ball about six days a week?" (confirm the number)
12. "Are you doing any weight training, strength, anything like that?"
13. "How would you say your nutrition is? Have you ever tracked your grams of protein?"
14. "Do you play for your high school? JV or varsity?"
15. "Tell me about your club situation." / "What position? How tall are you?"
16. "Any showcases or ID camps coming up this summer?"
17. "Do you know what you want to major in?"

### 4. Recruitment so far
18. "Kind of give me a rundown - what have you guys done as far as recruitment goes so far?"
19. "Have we sent emails? Do we have a highlight reel? A YouTube channel? A soccer Instagram?"
20. "Have we developed any relationship with any of these programs? Tell me about that."

### 5. The dream and the fit
21. "What is the dream outcome for you? Where do you want to go?"
22. "Why D1? Tell me more about that." / "How long has that been a dream of yours?" (McKenzie)
23. "Location, climate, distance from home - what's the perfect fit? Where are you willing to go,
    where are you not?" (McKenzie, Trinity)
24. To the parents: "Anything else that's like, this has to be here for this to make sense for
    us?" (McKenzie)

### 6. Money path - before the pitch
25. "What is your guys' current plan to pay for college right now?" (Seeleigh, Payton)
26. After their answer and the tuition / scholarship numbers: "Do we have a path to pay for that?"
    (Arevalo)

### 7. The gap
27. "We do not have any offers on the table currently, correct? Why do you guys think that is?"
    (Brooke)
28. "What's the biggest thing holding you guys back from driving forward with recruitment right
    now?" (Zimerman, Barrales)
29. "Is it the time that's hard, or is it the plan and the what-to-do that's hard?" (Barrales)
30. "Are you guys feeling confident navigating that process on your own right now?" (Zimerman)
31. To the parent: "What is your biggest fear with where [athlete] is at right now?" (Barrales)

### 8. Willingness and the hard truth
32. "What are you willing to do to get to the level you want?" (Brooke)
33. If the route may be JUCO or a lower level first: "Is that something we're willing to do, if
    push comes to shove?" (Brooke, Stoecklin, McKenzie)
34. Urgency from the real calendar - June 15, upcoming showcases. "Eighth grade is really ninth
    grade, especially on the girls' side." (Seeleigh, Trinity)

### 9. What it would mean
35. To the athlete: "What would it mean to you to get offers to play in college?"
36. To each parent: "And what would that mean for you, as the parent?" (Arevalo, Zimerman,
    Barrales)
37. "What is the number one thing that needs to be here, in this kind of program, for it to be a
    win for you guys?" (Zimerman, Barrales) - then build the pitch around their answer.

### 10. Commitment gate - athlete, then parents
38. "On a scale of 1 to 10, 1 being nah, 10 being 100%, how committed are you right now to this
    process of making this dream happen?" (McKenzie)
39. Anything under 10: "Tell me where that hesitation is." Then "Is it the fear of what it
    entails, or is it you don't know if that's what you want?" (McKenzie, Payton)
40. "Because I really can't move forward if we're not like at a 10." (McKenzie)
41. "And parents, you guys would say that's for sure as well?" (Zimerman)

### 11. Diagnosis and the offer
42. "What you guys really need is the clear guidance, the blueprint, and the map to get it done.
    Would you agree?" (Barrales)
43. "I definitely know I can help you. I'd love to offer you a spot. I'll walk you through what
    that looks like - if you have questions, raise your hand, holler at me. Sound good?"

### 12. The program
44. Your story - recruited five times, JUCO to D1.
45. The 3 pillars, with "Does that make sense?" after each:
    - Self-promotion (the World Cup player: "be your best promoter")
    - Sellability ("coaches are essentially purchasing you")
    - Outreach ("we write every single email")
46. How the coaching works - weekly 1:1, then every other week, start to sign; DMs and the
    community; parents only needed on decision calls.

### 13. Questions and proof
47. "What questions do you guys have so far?" Then ask the athlete separately: "[Athlete], any
    questions on that?" (McKenzie, Rappe)
48. If they ask about proof or trust, screen-share the community live: "That's my reference for
    you." (McKenzie)
49. "Will you be able to commit to a weekly Zoom call? And you're not going to join and then ghost
    us?" (Barrales, Payton)

### 14. Preview the next steps, then the price
50. Before the number: "It would just be sending over the payment link, getting that taken care
    of, then sending you an onboarding email and booking our first meeting for Monday morning."
    (Brooke [30:13])
51. "So you guys want to know what it takes to get started?"
52. "The investment is $8,000." **Stop talking.**
53. "Is that something you're able to do?"
54. If they ask what it covers: "Start to sign. You stay in the program until you sign. Nothing
    else." (Arevalo, Zimerman, Payton)

### 15a. They say yes
55. "You got the card on hand you want to use?"
56. Payment link into the chat or by message, live.
57. While they pay: athlete email into the chat, onboarding email, Skool join on the call, first
    meeting booked within 1-3 days ("What time do you go to school?"), homework for that day.

### 15b. Hesitation
58. "How are you feeling about that?" (Barrales, Erin)
59. "Is it more than you were expecting, or more than you're able to do?"
60. "Most of my families put it on a credit card up front. Is that something you'd be able to do?"
61. "The other thing I could do is a split pay - half down, half in 30 days. Would that work?"
62. Can't do the split: "Where would you say your credit is - good, fair, excellent?" Then
    Klarna / Affirm, or the 4-payment internal plan.
63. "Talk to my husband / wife":
    - First, get them on now: "Do you want to call him real quick and see if we can patch him
      back on?" (Brooke)
    - Or: "What do you think your wife needs to hear to arrive at the same yes that we did?"
      (Stoecklin)
    - Or the deposit: "Within 24 hours we forget 50% of what was discussed... would it be okay if
      we just move forward with that first payment so we could get everything started?" (Beth)
64. "Tell me, what do you need help thinking about?" / "I feel like we're right there. What's the
    hesitation for us right now?" (Zimerman)

### 15c. They still won't decide today
65. "When do you guys think you'll be able to sit down as a family and talk about it?" (Zimerman)
66. Book it with every decision-maker on it, on the calendar, before you hang up: "So then when
    should I put it on my end to check in with you guys, to make sure nothing falls through the
    cracks on my end?" (Seeleigh)
67. Move the existing invite to that time. (Stoecklin, Zimerman)
68. Never end on "if I don't hear from you by X." That is how all 6 lost calls ended, or worse.
