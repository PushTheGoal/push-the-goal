## TIMING

- Discovery done by about 25 min.
- Offer at about 30-35 min.
- Price at about 31-47 min. Closes landed between minute 27 and 47.
- Call done by about 50-60 min.
- On the lost calls that ran 72 minutes, the price landed at minute 63-64. Hold the pace.

## STEP 0 - Before the call

1. **Booking message** [v2]: "Looking forward to our call. One thing I ask of every family:
   both parents (or whoever helps make decisions for [athlete]) need to be on, on camera, with
   [athlete]. If that time doesn't work for everyone, message me and we'll move it."
2. Write every name on a sticky note: athlete, each parent, club, school. [v2 - Guyader's mom was
   called "Mike"]
3. Know their grade, GPA, club and league from the form so you can confirm instead of ask.

## STEP 1 - Open (0-3 min)

4. "Can you hear me and see me okay?"
5. "Tell me how to say your name so I don't embarrass myself." [J/M/A - Barrales]
6. ★ "Where are you guys calling in from?" Keep rapport short.
   - Find one real personal tie: club, hometown, mutual player, homeschool, faith.
   - If they're a faith family: "Do you mind if I just pray for us really quick?" [J/M/A - Carine]
7. Confirm what you know: "And then [grade] with a [GPA], correct?" Praise what's real ("Good
   student.").
8. "Right now still a good time? You'll have about 45 minutes?" [J/M/A - Barrales, Zimerman]

### Decision-maker check - every call, before the frame
9. ★ "Before we jump in - does anyone else need to be on the call who's going to be part of making
   a decision, that it would be helpful for them to hear what it looks like?"
   - [v2] and [J/M/A - Barrales]. On the lost calls this was asked 0 of 6 times.
10. If someone is missing:
    - "Does [mom/dad] want to pop in so they get the overview too? I want to not have to relay the
      information twice." [J/M/A - Barrales, Seeleigh]
    - If they can't join now: "Can we get them on by phone for the last 15 minutes?" [v2]
    - If they can't join at all: run discovery, then book the offer call with both of them before
      you pitch. [v2]
11. If the athlete is missing: "[Athlete] is gonna need to be on the call, for sure." [J/M/A -
    Zimerman]

## STEP 2 - Frame (by min 3)

12. ★ "What I want to do, guys, is deep dive on your situation - where you're at, what you're
    struggling with and where you need the most help. As long as I know for a fact I can help
    you, I'll offer you a spot in the program and show you how it works. If I don't feel like
    we're the right fit, I'll just point you in the best direction I see fit for your situation.
    Does that sound good?"

## STEP 3 - Why we're here (3-6 min)

13. ★ "[Athlete], why don't you start by telling me - what's the main reason we're on the call
    today?"
14. ★ "Parents, what do you guys see? I want to hear your perspective, it's very valuable here."
    [v2 and J/M/A - Barrales]
15. "What made you book the call?" [v2 - Silvati only gave the real reason in the last 3 minutes]
16. "How long have you been wanting to play college soccer?" [J/M/A - McKenzie, Trinity, Seeleigh]

## STEP 4 - Where they're at (6-15 min)

### Recruitment
17. ★ "Catch me up - what have you guys done as far as recruitment, if anything?"
18. "How many schools have you emailed? How often?" [v2]
19. "Are you sending film? Do we have a highlight reel? Is it on YouTube?"
20. "Do you have a soccer Instagram? How often do you post on it?" [J/M/A - Barrales, Zimerman,
    Stoecklin]
21. "When you say you've talked to coaches - have you had actual phone calls, or is it all emails?"
    [v2]
22. "Have we developed any relationship with any of these programs? Tell me about that."
    [J/M/A - McKenzie]
23. Juniors and up: "On June 15th and the days after, did any school reach out saying they want to
    talk?" [v2 - Christiansen]
24. "What are your strongest leads right now?" [v2]
25. "Any ID camps or showcases coming up this summer?" [J/M/A - Brooke, McKenzie, Seeleigh]

### Training and the athlete
26. ★ "What does your training week look like? Give me the full picture." Then confirm: "So we're
    on the ball about six days a week?"
27. "How many days are you on the ball outside team training?" [v2]
28. "Any weight training or strength work?" [J/M/A - Garza, Arevalo, Barrales, Trinity]
29. "What did breakfast look like today?" / "Have you ever tracked your grams of protein?"
    [v2 and J/M/A]
30. "High school - JV or varsity? Club - what level? What position?"
31. "Any injuries?" [v2 and J/M/A - McKenzie]
32. "What's the biggest weakness in your game right now?" [v2]
33. "Do you know what you want to major in? What inspired that?" [v2 and J/M/A]

### Other options - ask here, not after the price
34. "Have you guys looked at or worked with any other recruiting services? What did you think?"
    - From [v2].
    - On [J/M/A], 4 of the 6 lost calls had a competitor that was never addressed (NCSA on
      Sophie's call that night, Zev, Sports Recruits, another coach).

## STEP 5 - The dream (15-20 min)

35. ★ "What's the dream? Tell me what your dream is - not what you think you can do."
35b. "What makes this a successful outcome for you? Division, distance, academics, major?" [v2]
    - If they light up while answering: "You started smiling when you talked about that. Tell me
      more." [J/M/A - Payton]
36. "Why D1? Tell me more about that." / "How long has that been a dream of yours?" [J/M/A -
    McKenzie]
37. "Location, climate, distance from home - where are you willing to go, and where are you not?"
    [J/M/A - McKenzie, Trinity]
38. "If you found a program that was the right fit - coach, environment, major, you felt supported -
    would that still be a win, even if it wasn't [D1/dream school]?" [v2]
39. To the parents: "Anything that's like, this has to be here for this to make sense for us?"
    [J/M/A - McKenzie]

## STEP 6 - The gap: let them name it (20-27 min)

40. ★ "What do you guys feel is the biggest obstacle between where you are now and having those
    offers?" Ask the athlete, then the parents.
41. If they have no offers: "We do not have any offers on the table currently, correct? Why do you
    guys think that is?" [J/M/A - Brooke]
42. "Is it the time that's hard, or the plan and the what-to-do that's hard?" [J/M/A - Barrales]
43. "Are you guys feeling confident navigating this on your own?" / "Do you feel like we have a
    solid recruitment strategy right now?" [J/M/A - Zimerman, Seeleigh]
44. ★ "What's your plan right now to fix that?" [v2 - Rodarte, Watson]
45. "Where's that belief block coming from?" then "When your confidence drops, what actually
    happens?" Go two layers deep. [v2]
46. "Do you guys have a plan for the next four weeks before [their camp/date]?" [v2]
47. To the parent: "What is your biggest fear with where [athlete] is at right now?" Then the
    inverse: "How would you feel knowing you've got a plan and a path?" [J/M/A - Barrales]
48. **Cost of waiting:** "If the next six months look like the last six, where does that leave
    you on June 15th?" [v2]
49. **Urgency from the real calendar:** June 15, their showcases, and "Eighth grade is really ninth
    grade, especially on the girls' side." [J/M/A - Seeleigh, Trinity]
49b. **Coach on the call, with permission:** "Can you guys allow me to put on my coaching hat for a
    second here and be a coach?" Then give them the honest route. [J/M/A - Stoecklin]
    - Or the hard truth with a deadline: "I'm going to be brutally honest with you guys because I
      want to help you get where you want to go. We need to close on a Division I offer within the
      next three months, or we have to pivot our timeline." [J/M/A - Brooke]
    - If status is driving the dream, use the forced choice: D1 and riding the bench, or D2/NAIA and
      playing - "Which one are you choosing?" [J/M/A - Stoecklin]
    - If the athlete says they struggle to talk to coaches, use the call itself as proof: "You're
      talking to me right now, right? And I'm a coach." [J/M/A - Payton]
50. **Willingness:** "What are you willing to do to get to that level?" If the route may be JUCO
    or a lower level first: "Is that something we're willing to do, if push comes to shove?"
    [J/M/A - Brooke, McKenzie, Stoecklin]
51. ★ **Label it back:** "So right now you're dealing with [X], you want [goal] by [date], and
    what's missing is the blueprint and the accountability. Would you say that's accurate? Anything
    I'm missing?" Wait for "that's accurate." [v2 and J/M/A - Barrales]
    - If they say "we're already doing a lot," agree and reframe: "You've built the machine. What
      we do is make it consistent and targeted." [v2]
    - Do NOT agree that they don't need you. On Hope's call you said "this is not the level of
      support that you need overall," and the mom then said "I would be stoked to go with you."
      [J/M/A]

## STEP 7 - What it would mean (27-30 min)

52. ★ "[Athlete], what would it mean to you to find the right fit and get a scholarship to keep
    playing?"
53. ★ "[Parent], what would it mean to you?" Ask each parent separately.
54. Ask the quiet parent directly: "[Name], you've been quiet over there - what's important to you
    in this?" [v2 - Bash]
55. "How would it feel to actually have a plan this time around?"
56. Put a number on it if scholarship money matters to them: "I have a kid who just got $19,000 a
    year - almost $80,000. What would that mean to you guys?" [J/M/A - Zimerman]
57. "What is the number one thing that needs to be in a program like this for it to be a win for
    you guys?" Build the offer around their answer. [J/M/A - Zimerman, Barrales]
58. If the athlete's answer is soft: "I don't hear much conviction yet, and that's okay - I just
    need to know this is something you really want, not something you feel you have to do."
    [v2 - Brooks]

## STEP 8 - Money and decision process (before the pitch)

59. ★ "What's your guys' plan right now to pay for college?" [v2 - Melhem; J/M/A - Seeleigh,
    Payton]
60. If scholarship money matters, use real tuition and average scholarship numbers, then: "Do we
    have a path to pay for that?" [J/M/A - Arevalo]
61. "When you invest in something like this for [athlete], how do you two usually make the
    decision?" [v2]
62. If scholarships aren't their goal, sell development and fit, not the dollar math. [v2]

**Pick the right program here, before the pitch.** [J/M/A]

- On the lost calls, 9th graders Gaby and Alejandra got the $8K pitch after being told they were

## STEP 9 - Commitment, before the pitch

63. ★ "Not where you are right now - how committed are you to doing the work this takes? One is no
    way, 10 is 100% all in."
    - The "not where you are right now" wording is from [v2]. Kyla and Karis misread the scale as
      "rate your progress."
64. If they say 10: "Good answer. I needed to hear that from you." [v2]
65. If under 10: ★ "What would make it a 10?" [v2], or "Tell me where that hesitation is - is it the
    fear of what it entails, or you're not sure it's what you want?" [J/M/A - McKenzie, Payton]
    - ⚠ **DECIDE:**
      - v2 says never say "that's incredibly low" or "I wouldn't let you in." Those deflated the
        room on non-closes.
      - Jan/Mar/Apr used "Wow, that's very low" (Payton) and "I really can't move forward if we're
        not at a 10" (McKenzie), and both closed.
      - Recommend: keep the gate, drop the judgment. "I need to know this is a 10 for you before I
        offer you a spot."
66. "And parents, you'd say that's for sure as well?" [J/M/A - Zimerman, McKenzie]
67. "Would you be willing to show up to a weekly call and roll out an action plan every week? And
    you're not going to join and then ghost us?" [v2 and J/M/A - Barrales, Payton]

## STEP 10 - The offer (30-40 min)

68. ★ "Guys, I can tell 100% you're the right fit. I'd love to work with you. Let me walk you
    through how it works - if you have questions, raise your hand, holler at me. Sound good?"
69. **Story** - match it to their situation: injury, homeschool, late start, no big club, keeper,
    JUCO. Keep it short. Recruited five times, JUCO to D1.
70. **Pillar 1, Self-Promotion:** the World Cup player, "be your best promoter." Tie it to something
    they told you. Then "Does that make sense?"
71. **Pillar 2, Sellability:** "coaches are essentially purchasing you," film, personal statement
    video, "person over player." Lead with the money math only if STEP 8 said scholarships matter.
    Then "Does that make sense?"
72. **Pillar 3, Outreach:** "we write every single email," volume, consistency, follow-up, a
    specific four-week rotation. Then "Does that make sense?"
73. **Structure:** weekly 1:1, parent recaps, DM access, the community, parents only on decision
    calls, "start to sign."
    - ⚠ **DECIDE:**
      - v2 says "6 months of weekly 1:1 calls."
      - Jan/Mar/Apr says weekly, then every other week, then at least monthly, until signed.
      - Use one version.
74. **Proof, inside the offer:** screen-share the Skool community - real families, real wins.
    "That's my reference for you." [v2 - Navarro, Brooks; J/M/A - Carine, McKenzie, Zimerman]
75. **Guarantee, inside the offer, the same words every time:** [INSERT CONFIRMED TERMS - see Part
    5].
    - ⚠ **DECIDE:**
      - v2 says state it plainly. Holding it back cost Peterson, and it was Christiansen's deciding
        reason.
      - Jan/Mar/Apr held it back until asked ("I don't even like to bring that up") and still
        closed Carine and McKenzie.
      - Recommend v2: state it once, plainly.
75b. **Solve one of their live problems on the call before the price.** Draft the film email to the
    club, review their Instagram, coach the ID camp plan. Showing the skill is what the closes had
    in common. [J/M/A - Trinity, Isabella, Callah, Beth]
76. ★ "I went fast. What questions do you guys have?" Then ask the athlete separately: "[Athlete],
    any questions?"

## STEP 11 - Preview the next steps, then the price

77. Before the number: "From here it would just be sending over the payment link, getting that

79. ★ "The investment is $8,000. You stay in until you get the result you want." Then stop talking.
80. ★ "Is that something you're able to do?"
81. If they ask what's included: "Start to sign. Nothing else. That's it." [J/M/A]

## PART 3 - OBJECTIONS

### "We've been burned before" / trust

18. "I appreciate you keeping it real with me. Let me show you my community." Then screen-share.
    [v2 and J/M/A]
19. "It's not about the money. It's about the investment. It's the fact that it stings - because
    then I've got a serious commitment from you." [v2 - Navarro]

### "Is it worth it?" / "Will this actually work for her?"
20. The guarantee, stated plainly. Don't argue the number. [v2 - Christiansen]
21. "Where would you project her?" Give an honest level with a contrast so it's believable: "I told
    a family this morning NAIA. For her, 100% Division I." [v2 - Emersyn]

### "What if she ends up at [the school we already know] anyway?"
22. "That's a very risky move - all the eggs in one basket. You never know until you've had the
    experience and the offers are on the table." [v2 - Brooks]

### "How is this different from NCSA?"
23. "A lot of our students actually come from NCSA. They do lots of sports; we only do soccer. The
    difference is the one-to-one - you're not waiting an hour on a Zoom, you just message me." [v2]

### "The athlete isn't driving it" / the parent is doing the work
24. Agree with the parent, then turn to the athlete with the 1-10. Let the athlete answer the
    parent's worry. [v2 - Watson, Emersyn]
25. Position yourself as the outside voice: "They're going to hear it better from someone else.
    It's nothing on you." [J/M/A - Whiteside]

### "Is this too early?" / younger athlete
26. Offer Junior Prep or the downsell with an upgrade path. Don't hand them "too early" as a reason
    to wait - Brea's mom reused it: "are we doing it too early?" [J/M/A]

### They still won't decide today
27. ★ "When do you guys think you'll be able to sit down as a family and talk about it?" [J/M/A -
    Zimerman]
28. Book it on the calendar with every decision-maker: "So when should I put it on my end to check
    in with you, to make sure nothing falls through the cracks on my end?" [J/M/A - Seeleigh]
    - Move the existing invite. [J/M/A - Stoecklin, Zimerman]
29. Don't ask a first-meeting family to decide in 20 minutes after they've said money isn't the
    issue. Book the follow-up with a time instead. [v2]
30. **Never end on "if I don't hear from you by X."**
    - All 7 deferred closes in Jan/Mar/Apr ended with a booked day and time.
    - All 6 lost calls ended without one.

## PART 4 - STOP DOING

From v2:
- Pitching with a decision-maker missing.
- "That's incredibly low" / "I wouldn't let you in" when an athlete misreads the 1-10.
- "I feel like you don't want that" (Guyader) and "So you think it's a scam?" (Silvati).
- "Go look around, go see" (Walling). Say instead: "Who else are you looking at? Let's compare
  right now."
- Asking a first-meeting family to decide in 20 minutes when money isn't the issue.
- Refusing a written summary. Send a one-page recap and book the follow-up in the same move.
- Holding back the guarantee, or changing its terms mid-sentence.
- Using different numbers for the same thing (93 vs 170 students).
- Getting names wrong.

From Jan/Mar/Apr:
- **Letting the call run long.** Price at minute 63-64 on the two 72-minute lost calls.
- **Disqualifying a warm family.**
  - Cooper's dad asked for "a hybrid." You said "you guys don't need that top level support,"
    then gave the strategy away free.
  - Offer the right-sized option instead.
- **Agreeing they don't need you** (Hope).
- **Pitching $8K to an athlete you just told is early** (Gaby, Alejandra). Offer Junior Prep or the
  downsell.
- **Quoting the next program three different ways** (Brea).
- **No answer to "what's your success rate?"** (Sophie). Use the confirmed claim plus the
  community screen-share.
- **Letting a competitor sit unaddressed** (Sophie had an NCSA call that night).
- **Ending without a booked next step.**
- **Saying the price while a decision-maker isn't paying attention.** On Seeleigh's call the dad
  had to ask "What did she say?"

## PART 5 - CONFIRM BEFORE THESE GO BACK INTO THE SCRIPT

Every one of these was said on a recorded call, in different words. Settle one version you can
back up, then drop it into STEP 10 and the objection answers.

1. **The guarantee - exact terms.** Versions on record:
   - Money back at the end of the year if no scholarship (Emersyn).
   - A scholarship never less than the investment (Edwards).
   - "I guarantee scholarship back in investment" (Stoecklin).
   - "At least five offers... I don't guarantee this part yet" (Edwards).
   - "No refund if you quit halfway" (Brooks).
   - "I do offer a money back guarantee, but I don't even like to bring that up" (Carine).
   - It is written into the agreement (McKenzie).
   - "Money-back guarantee" promised to Isabella.
2. **"100% success rate."** Said on Watson, Bash, Brooks, Arevalo and Zimerman. On Arevalo and
   Zimerman it was explained as "I only take committed families."
3. **"Average about five offers."** Said on McKenzie and Seeleigh. Karis Edwards' mom pushed back:
   "I don't know anybody that's gotten five offers this year."
4. **Student count.** 93 (Peterson, June); 170 (Guyader, July); about 25 active (Bash); "27 right
   now, capped around 30... full program about 90-92" (McKenzie, April).
