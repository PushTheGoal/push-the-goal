The Honest Picture

Lauren is a junior, so this is the year that decides it, and right now there is no plan, no outreach, and no film. A former club coach deleted her Instagram out of spite when the family left for ECNL, and it took every highlight she had with it. That is a setback, not a sentence. The family is new to recruiting and there is a language barrier, so they have been guessing off Instagram and conflicting advice. The fix is a clear blueprint, rebuilding the film from zero, and starting real outreach now before fall season gets crazy.

The 6-Month Build

  • Rebuild the film from scratch. The club does not reliably film, so mom and dad film every game on the phone starting now. No film, no recruitment.
  • Cut the first highlight reel fast. Use the few clips she still has plus new footage and get a reel live so we have something to send coaches.
  • Start outreach immediately. We draft every email for her, she follows the templates, and we build the volume the junior window demands.
  • Chase the biggest scholarship, not the biggest logo. Open D1, D2, and NAIA at the same time so the best money wins. NAIA and juco can stack academic plus athletic dollars like a D1.
  • Lead with the student. A 3.7 with a 99 in Medical Terminology is money on the table - we market the academics as hard as the soccer.

The Athlete's Edge

Lauren is a pure playmaker - team assist leader with 50-plus in a season and a stack of tournament championships. That vision translates at every level. Pair it with a 3.7 and a real science path and she becomes a low-risk, high-reward recruit who unlocks academic money on top of athletic money. And the story sells: a kid who lost all her film to a vindictive coach and rebuilt her whole recruitment from zero. That is exactly who coaches bet on.

Target Criteria (No Named Schools Yet - We Build The List)

  • Texas or a surrounding state. Close to family is a family non-negotiable - we build the list inside that radius.
  • Biggest scholarship possible, division-agnostic. D1, D2, and NAIA all in play. The money and the fit decide, not the label on the jersey.
  • Offers her science path. Biology / health science / kinesiology toward sonography - the school has to fit the degree, not just the soccer.
  • Responsive coaches. We rank by who actually answers and push everyone to a yes or no so she is not chasing ghosts.
  • Juco as a strategic lane. Brookhaven College (strong transfer-out pipeline in Texas) is a real option to get started, cover cost, and transfer up.

The 3 Pillars

  • Self-Promotion: Lauren learns to be her own best promoter - her story, her film, and her academics in front of coaches every week.
  • Sellability: We package the playmaking, the assists, the 3.7, and the character so a coach sees a full-ride-worthy recruit, not another email.
  • Outreach: 100+ personalized touches with relentless follow-up. We write them for her first, then she owns it. Visibility is a numbers game.

Personal Brand & Film - Rebuild From Zero

The old coach took the Instagram and the highlights with it. Good. We build it back better and prove she never needed him. New Instagram, new reel, new footage filmed by the family every single game. We also chase down any ECNL film the club has by requesting access, and stitch together everything from this past season. The volume of film is what turns her assists into offers.

Ownership & The Family

Oscar and Carla are all-in and want to do this right, and the language barrier just means Lauren interprets and owns more of the process, which is exactly what coaches recruit. Parents film and support; Lauren sends and follows up. The commitment is there - she rated it near the top. We hold the standard and keep the whole family on the same page through the app.
