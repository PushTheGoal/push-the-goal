## Who it is for

Two viewers, and every piece picks one as the main target (ledger column `audience`):

1. **The athlete** - high school soccer player, roughly 14-18, wants to play in college, does not
   know how recruiting actually works, assumes being good is enough.
2. **The parent** - pays, worries, is out of their depth on the process, scared of wasting the
   window and the money on showcases that go nowhere.

Younger players (Junior Prep age) are a third, smaller target - skill development content.

## What every piece is trying to do

The funnel: **non-follower sees a trial reel -> follows -> sees carousels that teach -> comments a
keyword or DMs RECRUIT -> strategy call.** Content earns the follow and the trust. It never sells
on the post itself.

## Voice

Coach Izi on camera and in text. The full rules are in `CLAUDE.md` (Voice + the warmth
correction) and `skills/push-the-goal-call-notes/references/voice.md`. For content specifically:

- Direct, intense, specific. A real coach telling you what actually happens, not a motivational page.
- Warmth correction applies: hard truth, then why it works in their favour. Never mock a kid,
  never shame a parent. "Most players do X" is fine. "You're lazy" is not.
- Short sentences. One idea per slide, one idea per reel.
- No em-dashes (spaced hyphens). "Message", not "text". "Athlete" or "player", never "child".
- No emojis in on-screen text or slide copy. Captions may use **at most one** emoji, never as
  decoration on every line. (Lead-mag email subject lines keep their own emoji rule.)
- No clichés: "level up", "grind never stops", "dream big", "unlock your potential", "game-changer".
- Faith: natural and occasional, mainly in `mindset`. Never forced onto a recruiting tip.

Trial reels lean on **Follow** - the viewer does not know us yet. Carousels lean on save, share
and the keyword.

Lead magnet keywords already in use: see `skills/weekly-lead-mag-vault/SKILL.md` (EMAIL, FILM,
LIST, SPRINT, PARENT and others). Only use one if its DM automation is confirmed live - a keyword
that sends nothing burns trust.

## Hard rules - never broken

- **No prices.** Name Soccer Recruitment 101 or Junior Prep Program, never a number.
- **No invented facts.** No made-up stats ("90% of players..."), schools, scholarship amounts,
  NCAA rules or dates. A rule or date gets checked against the current NCAA source before it ships,
  or it does not ship.
- **Minors on camera.** A student or athlete appears (face, name, school, jersey) only if the asset
  library marks written consent from the parent as on file. No consent mark = not used. Never
  name a minor's school, city or grad year without that consent.
- **Proof is real.** A commitment, offer or result is only claimed if it is confirmed and the
  family said yes to sharing it.
- **No client calls as content.** Coaching calls and Strategy Sessions are private. Nothing from a
  Fathom recording becomes content. Izi records content separately.
- **No health or personal details**, same as everywhere else in Push the Goal.
- **No guarantees.** Never "get a scholarship", "get signed in 90 days". The program promises the
  work and the system, not the outcome.
- **No other brands' content.** No reposting other creators' videos or audio.
