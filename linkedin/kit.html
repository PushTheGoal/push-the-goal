About
I speak to athletes, teams, schools, parents and faith communities about the long game: resilience, ownership, and a confidence that holds when the applause gets quiet.

My path to Division I soccer was anything but straight. I didn't start club soccer until I was 14. Two ACL tears, a quad tear, five knee surgeries, COVID disruptions and eligibility obstacles. At one point everything I owned was packed in my car, and I still had a game to play that night. I made it to D1 anyway.

That story is the foundation of everything I do.

KEYNOTE SPEAKING
Talks for athletes, coaches, parents, schools and faith communities:
- Setbacks Don't Rewrite Your Story - resilience after injury and loss
- Confidence vs. Arrogance - building self-trust that doesn't depend on praise
- Talent Isn't Enough - ownership, discipline and the habits that get you noticed
- Leaders Ask for Help - why strength doesn't mean carrying it alone

THE LONG GAME PODCAST
I co-host The Long Game Podcast with Maggie Gehne: real conversations on faith, fitness, wellness and relationships for women building a life that lasts.

COLLEGE SOCCER RECRUITMENT
For the past 10 years I've helped athletes take their game to the next level. Through Push the Goal Soccer, I give high school players and their families a clear recruiting plan and the mentorship to follow through, from getting found to getting signed.
