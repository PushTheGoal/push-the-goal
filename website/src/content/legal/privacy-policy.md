**Last updated: September 2026**


Push The Goal ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we
collect on push-the-goal.com and through our coaching programs, how we use it, and the
choices you have. It applies to every visitor, every parent who contacts us, and every
athlete we coach.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you fill out a form, book a call, enroll in a program, or
message us, we collect what you provide: your name, email address, phone number, your
athlete's name, grade, position, club and school, and anything you write in a message.

**Information we collect while coaching.** During a program we keep the notes that make the
coaching work: goals, weekly targets, school lists, highlight video links, academic
information you share, and a record of our sessions and check-ins.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to see
which pages are visited and how the site performs. It collects your device type, browser,
approximate location, and the pages you view. It does not collect your name.

## Information about children

Junior Prep serves athletes in grades 5 through 8, and many are under 13. Here is how we
handle that:

- A parent or legal guardian enrolls the athlete and is our client. We collect information
  about an athlete under 13 from the parent, not from the child through our website.
- Weekly check-ins with a Junior Prep athlete happen with the parent's knowledge. A parent can
  join any session, see any message, and ask what we have on file at any time.
- We collect only what the coaching needs. We do not ask children for information beyond their
  soccer, school and goals.
- A parent can review the information we hold about their child, ask us to correct it, or ask
  us to delete it by emailing izi@push-the-goal.com. We will do so, except where we must keep
  a record for billing or legal reasons.

If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's involvement, tell us and
we will remove it.

## How we use your information

We use it to:

- Answer your questions and schedule calls.
- Provide the coaching program you enrolled in.
- Send appointment reminders, session notes, and program updates.
- Bill you for the program you chose.
- Send occasional marketing messages about our programs and content. You can opt out at any
  time.
- Understand how our website is used so we can improve it.

## Text messages

By giving us your phone number and checking the consent box on our forms, you agree to
receive text messages from Push The Goal about appointments, reminders, support, follow-ups,
and occasional promotions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply
STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Your consent to receive texts is never shared
with or sold to anyone for their marketing.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own
marketing. We share it only with the services that run our business, and only what they need:

- Our website analytics provider (Google Analytics).
- The platform that runs our booking pages, forms, email and text messages.
- Our payment processor, which handles card details directly. We never see or store your full
  card number.
- Video call software used for coaching sessions.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's
safety.

We do not share your athlete's information with college coaches, schools, clubs or recruiting
services. Outreach to coaches is done by your athlete and your family, with our guidance.

## Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to make the site work.
You can turn cookies off in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as well
without them.

## How long we keep it

We keep contact information while you are a client and for a reasonable time afterward so we
can answer questions and honor re-enrollment. Coaching notes are kept for the length of the
program and for a limited period after it ends, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your
information sooner.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your
information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot
promise absolute security, but we take it seriously.

## Your choices

- **Marketing:** unsubscribe from any email or reply STOP to any text.
- **Access and correction:** ask us what we hold about you or your athlete and we will show
  you and fix anything that is wrong.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your information and we will, except where we must keep a
  record for billing or legal reasons.
- **California residents** have the right to know, delete and opt out of the sale of personal
  information under California law. We do not sell personal information. Email us to
  exercise any right.

## Links to other sites

Our site links to other websites, including the NCAA and articles we recommend. Their privacy
practices are their own.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top tells you when it last changed. If a change is
significant, we will let current clients know by email.
