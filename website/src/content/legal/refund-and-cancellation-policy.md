**Last updated: November 2026**


Push The Goal sells coaching, so there is nothing to ship back. This page explains how
enrollment, cancellation and missed sessions work. It applies to every program we offer,
including College Soccer Recruiting Coaching and Junior Prep.

## Free calls

The strategy call and the Junior Prep intro call are free. There is nothing to refund and
nothing to cancel. If you cannot make it, rebook through the same link or email us.

## No refunds

All program purchases are final. When you enroll, your athlete's spot is held for the whole
season or billing period and the coaching plan is built for them from day one, so we do not
offer refunds, in full or in part, once a purchase is made. Please use the free call to ask
every question you have before you enroll.

## Withdrawing From a Program

You may withdraw from a program at any time. This applies to voluntary withdrawal and to any
other form of withdrawal, for any reason and at any point in the program. Withdrawing does not
entitle you to a refund. Absolutely no refunds, in full or in part, are given for any withdrawal.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time to stop future charges. Email izi@push-the-goal.com.

- **Monthly programs** end at the close of the month you have already paid for. No further
  charges after that.
- **Season enrollments** (including Junior Prep) run to the end of the season you paid for.

## Re-enrolling

Re-enrollment is your choice every time. We will never charge you for a new season or a new
program without your say-so. When a season is ending, we will reach out and ask.

## Missed and rescheduled sessions

Life with a young athlete is busy. Give us at least 24 hours' notice and we will reschedule a
session at no cost. A session missed without notice counts as used. If Izi has to reschedule,
you always get the session back.

## Billing questions

If something looks wrong on your statement, email us first. We fix billing mistakes quickly.
A chargeback filed without contacting us may pause your athlete's program while the dispute
is reviewed.

## Questions

Email izi@push-the-goal.com, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM.
