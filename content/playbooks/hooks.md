# Hook library

The hook is the first 1-3 seconds of a reel and slide 1 of a carousel. Each hook below has a type
id - that id goes in the ledger's `hook_type` column, which is how the Sunday review learns which
kinds of hook actually win for this audience. Write fresh hooks from these patterns; never paste
the same hook twice in 30 days.

## Hook types

| Type id | Pattern | Example (Push the Goal) |
|---|---|---|
| `callout` | Name the viewer exactly | "If you're a 2028 grad with no college emails yet, watch this." |
| `hard-truth` | Say the thing nobody says | "College coaches are not coming to find you." |
| `myth` | Kill a belief they hold | "Showcases don't get you recruited. This does." |
| `mistake` | Name the error they are making | "The email mistake that gets you ignored by every coach." |
| `how-to` | Promise a usable outcome | "How to write a coach email that actually gets a reply." |
| `number` | A specific count | "3 things college coaches check before they ever watch your film." |
| `parent` | Speak straight to the parent | "Parents - stop doing this in the recruiting process." |
| `story` | Open on a moment | "I went through college recruitment five times. Here's what I'd do differently." |
| `question` | A question they can't ignore | "Would a college coach recruit you off your Instagram?" |
| `contrast` | Signed vs not signed | "Players who get signed do this. Players who wait don't." |
| `timeline` | Urgency by grad year | "If you're a junior and you haven't done this yet, it's time." |
| `pov` | Put them in the moment | "POV: a college coach just opened your highlight film." |

Facts inside a hook follow the brand rules: no invented numbers. "Five times through recruitment"
is Izi's own story and is true. A statistic needs a source or it does not go in.
