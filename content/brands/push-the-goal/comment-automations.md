# Instagram comment automations in GHL

Read-only map of the 15 "Instagram comment automation" workflows in the Push the Goal GHL
sub-account, taken 2026-10-27. Nothing was changed. Re-check before relying on it - Ifty and Izi
edit these.

## How they work (this decides what a caption can promise)

Every one is the same shape: **Instagram comment on a specific post -> reply to the comment ->
add tag -> DM** (some add a wait and a second DM). The trigger has four filters: the Instagram
account, an exact keyword, "published post", and **one or two specific posts**.

So a keyword only works on the exact posts it is pointed at. **A new post that says "Comment
RECRUIT" sends nothing** until a workflow is pointed at that post. Every post the content system
publishes with a comment CTA needs its workflow set up first.

Each keyword field shows two spellings (e.g. `RECRUIT`, `recruit`) plus "+1" hidden variant.

## What this means for the content system

1. **13 of 15 run on Izi's personal Instagram (izimurray_), not @pushthegoalsoccer.** Only 5 Recipe
   and ADS watch the Push the Goal account. Most of the comment-to-DM funnel lives on the personal
   page today.
2. **Live keywords:** RECRUIT (11 workflows), HOOKS, COMMIT, and BOOK and EMAIL, which are both broken.
   The lead-magnet words FILM, LIST, SPRINT and PARENT are **not** wired up as comment automations here.
3. **Keywords are tied to posts, not the account.** Any reworded or reposted piece that carries a
   comment CTA needs a workflow pointed at the new post the day it goes up. A repost is a new post.
4. **Broken:** BOOK LEAD and The College EMAIL V have no post selected.
5. **Duplicate:** Leads and Ig Leads watch the same post with the same keyword and message - a
   commenter on that post can get two DMs.
6. **Attribution:** every workflow tags `izi ig comment`; freebie ones add `rc email`. Nothing tags
   *which post* a lead came from, so the dashboard can't yet tie a lead to a post. A per-post or
   per-freebie tag would fix it.
7. **The two freebie links that drive most enrolments** - College Coach Email Vault (2,076) and the
   30-Day Posting Playbook (208) - are the strongest proven offers to reuse.
